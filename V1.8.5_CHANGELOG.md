# HINT Study Console v1.8.5

Changes from the supplied v1.8.4 project are intentionally limited to the requested participant-window and experimenter-console UI behavior:

- Every participant-facing activity opens fullscreen by default, including training/practice activities. The existing F11 testing toggle is unchanged.
- The experimenter console status bar now shows live activity elapsed time for every started activity. Existing Study 1 and Study 2 countdown/automatic completion behavior is unchanged and is shown alongside elapsed time. Study 3 and training show elapsed time only.
- Study 1 Requested Feedback displays a prominent "SYSTEM IS WAITING FOR YOUR FEEDBACK" banner while feedback is pending in both Gridworld and Continuous Room participant windows. The banner is hidden again as soon as the request is resolved or times out.

No RL, intervention, sensor-recording, storage, workflow, condition, or timing-limit logic was intentionally changed.
