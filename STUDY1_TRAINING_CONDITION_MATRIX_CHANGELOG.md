# Study 1 Training Condition Matrix — v0.5

Study 1 Training now mirrors the experimental Study 1 design.

## Required training conditions

Each participant must complete all eight practice combinations before Study 1 experimental collection:

- Requested Feedback × Keyboard
- Requested Feedback × Joystick
- Requested Feedback × Voice
- Requested Feedback × Eye Gaze
- Anytime Feedback × Keyboard
- Anytime Feedback × Joystick
- Anytime Feedback × Voice
- Anytime Feedback × Eye Gaze

## Behavior

- Study 1 Training has its own 2 × 4 status matrix.
- Training and experimental Study 1 status are independent (`practice=1` vs `practice=0`).
- Each cell shows Not Started, In Progress, Completed, or Needs Repeat.
- Select Next Incomplete works in both Training and Study panels.
- Repeating an already completed training condition requires confirmation.
- Study 1 experimental trials are blocked until Study 1 Training reaches 8/8.
- The workflow sidebar shows `x/8 conditions` for both Study 1 Training and Study 1 Study.
